# Curriculum Module

## Introduction
This curriculum module outlines the structure and content for the course.

## Course Objectives
1. To explore various musical styles.
2. To engage in improvisation.
3. To develop performance skills.

## Weekly Breakdown
- **Week 1:** Introduction to Musical Genres
- **Week 2:** Basics of Improvisation
- **Week 3:** Ensemble Performance

## Assessment
Students will be assessed based on participation, assignments, and final performance.
